29 August 2026

What changed
- Homepage positioning now leads with FACKTS as a platform group, not a media company.
- Hoops and Music are deliberately separated visually and in copy.
- Removed the mixed Hoops + Music VERSUS image collage from the homepage.
- Removed the media-heavy Featured Watch wall and Stories wall from the homepage.
- Proof now routes into the actual Hoops and Music platforms instead of dominating the corporate homepage.
- FACKTS properties are presented as upcoming formats built on top of the platforms.
- People section is text-first until admin has confirmed portraits.
- Partnership section is outcome-led and platform-led.
- Homepage image slots are centralized in lib/content.js as homeMedia for future admin/CMS control.
- Menu/footer interface typography now uses Barlow instead of the previous generic/mono treatment.

Font note
Acumin is proprietary Adobe software and is not bundled with this project. The interface uses Barlow as the current distinctive licensed web-safe replacement. If an Adobe Fonts kit for Acumin is supplied later, the --font-ui layer can be swapped without redesigning the site.
